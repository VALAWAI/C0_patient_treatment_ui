Frail-VIG fragility index (Spict scale)
It helps identify the most fragile people who have one or more health problems. It is based on the comprehensive geriatric assessment (applicable to non-geriatric patients) that evaluates areas such as functional independence, nutritional status, cognitive, emotional, social, geriatric syndromes (confusion syndrome, falls, ulcers, polypharmacy, dysphagia), symptoms (pain or dyspnea) and diseases oncological, respiratory, cardiac, neurological, digestive or renal).
